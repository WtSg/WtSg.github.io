# Surface brief: photography gallery, version "Contact sheet"

Scope: `/photography/contact-sheet/` (`_layouts/gallery-contact.html`), one of the gallery versions the owner is comparing. Visitor mode: Experience. Same audience, content and constraints as the main gallery brief (`.impeccable/surfaces/layouts-gallery-html.md`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gallery is a photographer's contact sheet on a desk: every frame at once, read through a loupe, the keepers ringed in grease pencil. It refuses the portfolio grid by making the whole roll the page and magnification the way in.

OWN-WORLD: Dark slate desk (#222527), a glossy white contact print, black film strips with sprocket holes and light edge numbers (Barlow Condensed), upright pictures lying sideways on the film, a black-rimmed loupe with a glass highlight, and red grease pencil (Rock Salt) for the margin note and the rings.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole sheet, moves the loupe over frames to look closer, picks a frame (it gets ringed), and studies the enlargement.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sheet centred on the desk. Top margin: the typed label (name links home) and "Just my life" in grease pencil. Below: the strip of frames with edge printing; the loupe resting over the newest frame, magnifying it.

FORM: Contact sheet and loupe, position 1 on the ordered list. seed:9646a7dab897f296. Signature interaction: the loupe is a live magnified copy of the sheet under the pointer; choosing a frame draws a hand-drawn grease-pencil ring, then opens the enlargement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
