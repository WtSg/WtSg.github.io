# Surface brief: photography gallery, version "Instant prints"

Scope: `/photography/instant/` (`_layouts/gallery-instant.html`), one of the gallery versions the owner is comparing. Visitor mode: Experience. Same audience, content and constraints as the main gallery brief (`.impeccable/surfaces/layouts-gallery-html.md`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gallery is a fridge door in daylight covered in instant prints of everyday life. It refuses the white portfolio grid and the black slideshow: the photos are objects held up by magnets, loosely, the way people really keep them.

OWN-WORLD: Sea-foam enamel (#9fd6c2) drenching the page with a soft sheen, a freezer-door seam, a chrome handle on the opening edge, round plastic magnets in tomato, cobalt, sunflower and black, instant prints with the thick bottom margin written on in felt pen (Kalam), and plastic letter magnets spelling the title (Rubik).

STORY: The visitor sees the newest print hanging large under the freezer door, scrolls down the door as each print slaps on and develops from murky blue-green to colour, and takes any print off the door to look at closely.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mint door. Top: the freezer door with "PHOTOGRAPHY" in crooked letter magnets and "Just my life · Wentao Shangguan" in felt pen, then the seam. Centre: the newest instant print, large, slightly rotated, held by a red magnet, developing. Right edge: the chrome handle.

FORM: Instant prints on a fridge, position 7 on the ordered list (the roll's assignment). seed:9646a7dab897f296. Signature interaction: the print slaps on, the magnet clacks, the picture emerges from instant-film murk; clicking a print lets the magnet go.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
