# Surface brief: photography gallery, version "Slide night"

Scope: `/photography/projector/` (`_layouts/gallery-projector.html`), one of the gallery versions the owner is comparing. Visitor mode: Experience; the photographs lead. Same audience, content and constraints as the main gallery brief (`.impeccable/surfaces/layouts-gallery-html.md`): true colours and aspect ratios, published watermarked copies only, no file names, very little text, no feed layout, reduced motion gets still states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gallery is a slide night at home: each photograph is thrown big onto a living-room wall by a slide projector. It refuses the white portfolio grid and the black full-screen slideshow by putting the room, the beam and the tray around the picture.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black warm room (#0b0907), tungsten beam with drifting dust, the picture itself with its light spilling onto the wall, a round slide tray seen from above with numbered slots and mount edges, a corded remote with rubber keys, pencil handwriting on slide mounts (Reenie Beanie) and molded-plastic numbers (Barlow Condensed). Below: a backlit white light table with every slide in a white plastic mount.

STORY: The visitor watches the first slide come into focus, scrolls (or presses the remote) to advance one slide at a time, and ends at the light table where every slide is visible and any can be projected or opened.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark room. The newest photograph projected large in the centre (about 64% of the viewport height), the beam fanning up to it from the bottom edge with dust in it. Top left the wordmark (name links home) and "Just my life" in pencil. Bottom left the slide tray, partly off the corner; next to it the slide's handwritten label. Bottom right the remote: previous, count, next, look closely, light table.

FORM: Slide projector, position 2 on the ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK; the roll assigned position 7). seed:9646a7dab897f296. Signature interaction: the slide change (shutter dark, tray turns one slot, the next slide drops in out of focus and the lens hunts into focus).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
