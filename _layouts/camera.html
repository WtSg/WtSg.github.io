How it focuses

Turn the lens to a distance, with the slider or by dragging across the drawing. The lens moves out; the cam at its back pushes the roller; the levers swing the mirror, and with it the small window's line of sight. Where the two lines of sight cross is where the lens is focused. At 1 m they meet on the subject, and in the finder the two images in the patch become one.

∞
